# 《點石齋畫報》〈瞽目復明〉（1892 年，巴伐利亞邦立圖書館 bsb00075646） / Gu mu fu ming [Sight Restored]: Transcription Dataset

1892 年《點石齋畫報》匏一第五圖〈瞽目復明〉報導福州一位長期失明的患者到濟世醫館求診，描述院內西醫診察、施治與報紙所記的轉歸。這則圖文可用於研究晚清福建的就醫經驗、西醫院形象與新聞如何報導患者。

Gu mu fu ming [Sight Restored], published in the Dianshizhai Pictorial in 1892, reports that a patient who had long been blind sought care at the Jishi Hospital in Fuzhou. The article recounts an examination, treatment and the outcome reported by the newspaper. It provides an illustrated source for the history of care seeking and the portrayal of Western hospitals in late Qing Fujian.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。原文按欄定位，篇題、版記、圖款和印記單獨記錄。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. Body text is addressable by column. The heading, artist's signature, seals and publication marks are recorded separately. Human proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement are pending. The actual model identifiers and usage are unknown.
